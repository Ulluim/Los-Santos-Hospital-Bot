## 1. Introduction
**AXDEVS** ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting information processed when you invite, add, or interact with our Discord bot (Los Santos Hospital Bot). This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed, how it is used, how long it is kept, and your choices regarding your data.

## 2. Information We May Process
Depending on the features and commands used in a server, the Bot may process:
* **Discord Identifiers:** User IDs, server (guild) IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, and message IDs necessary to respond to commands, apply permissions, or maintain server configurations.
* **Command Inputs & Configuration Data:** Arguments, settings, or parameters you explicitly provide when using Bot commands or configuring server features.
* **Operational & Diagnostic Logs:** Technical logs (such as timestamps, error traces, or command execution events) used to maintain stability, troubleshoot issues, and prevent abuse.

We do **not** intentionally collect sensitive personal information outside what is voluntarily provided through Discord interactions, nor do we sell your data to third parties for advertising.

## 3. How We Use Information
We use processed information solely to:
* Provide, operate, maintain, and improve the Bot's features and commands.
* Save and apply server-specific or user-specific settings where applicable.
* Monitor performance, fix bugs, and protect against spam, abuse, or Terms of Service violations.
* Comply with applicable laws or Discord's Developer Terms and Community Guidelines.

## 4. Data Storage & Retention
* **Temporary Processing:** Many interactions are processed in memory or transiently to execute a command without permanent storage.
* **Stored Data:** Any configuration data or records stored in a database or storage system are retained only for as long as needed to provide the service or until removal is requested or the Bot is removed from a server (subject to standard backup or log rotation cycles).

## 5. Data Sharing & Third Parties
We do not sell or rent user or server data. Data may only be shared or accessed when:
* Required to run the Bot's hosting and infrastructure providers under appropriate safeguards.
* Necessary to investigate security incidents, fraud, or violations of our Terms of Service or Discord policies.
* Required by applicable law or valid legal process.

## 6. Data Deletion & Your Rights
Server administrators or users may request information about or deletion of associated data (where applicable) by contacting **AXDEVS** using the contact method listed below. Please include relevant Discord IDs (such as your User ID or Server ID) so we can locate and process the request.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as the Bot evolves or legal/platform requirements change. The badge and date at the top indicate when the latest revisions were made.
